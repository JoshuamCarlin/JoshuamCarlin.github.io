Joshua Meza

Business Administration📚

Joshua Meza is a pre-business sophomore at the University of California, Riverside, with experience in student leadership, emergency preparedness, safety procedures, and community engagement. Based in Moreno Valley, California, he brings a detail-oriented approach to teamwork, equipment organization, and professional communication. His background combines practical training through the Moreno Valley Fire Department Explorer Program with hands-on leadership as the founder and president of the Valley View Hiking Club. Together, these experiences have shaped his interest in supporting organized, safety-conscious environments where preparation and accountability matter.[Attachment] From May 2024 through June 2026, Joshua participated in the Moreno Valley Fire Department Explorer Program, gaining introductory exposure to emergency-response practices and hazardous-materials incident operations. His training introduced him to scene procedures, sampling considerations, monitoring sensors, and the role of laboratory analysis in identifying hazards. He also practiced inspecting, putting on, using, and removing personal protective equipment, including structural firefighting and wildland gear. This experience helped him develop an understanding of how careful preparation and consistent procedures support individual and team safety.[Attachment] Joshua’s responsibilities included checking self-contained breathing apparatus equipment for readiness, reviewing cylinder air levels, and inspecting equipment condition before training activities. He learned foundational fire-science concepts and practiced basic patient assessment, including obtaining and documenting vital signs. Throughout training scenarios, medical responses, and public events, he communicated respectfully with team members and community members. These experiences gave him opportunities to strengthen his attention to detail, follow instructions carefully, and contribute to a collaborative environment.[Attachment] As founder and president of the Valley View Hiking Club from December 2023 through June 2025, Joshua organized hiking trips, beach outings, social events, and outdoor-skills workshops. His leadership extended beyond planning activities to preparing participants for safe and enjoyable experiences. He researched trails and conditions, communicated route information and hydration needs, and explained safety expectations before outings. He also maintained participant accountability before, during, and after activities, helping ensure that group members remained accounted for throughout each event.[Attachment] Managing equipment was another important part of Joshua’s leadership. He prepared, organized, transported, and tracked essential supplies, including first-aid materials, hydration resources, signaling equipment, batteries, and hiking poles. These responsibilities connected event coordination with practical inventory management and reinforced the importance of reliable preparation. His additional skills include data collection, documentation, supply tracking, customer service, and working both independently and as part of a team. He is also familiar with Google Workspace applications, including Docs, Drive, and Slides.[Attachment] Joshua has completed California Civil Rights Prevention Training and received City of Moreno Valley Special Mayoral Recognition. As he continues his studies at UC Riverside, he is interested in applying his background to opportunities that combine administrative support, safety awareness, and service. His professional interests include supporting Environmental Health and Safety programs through careful documentation, data collection, inventory assistance, and dependable teamwork. He aims to bring the same preparation, accountability, and respectful communication developed through his training and student leadership to his next professional opportunity, while continuing to build his knowledge and contribute meaningfully to the university community.
